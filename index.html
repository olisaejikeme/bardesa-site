What we've built so far

Wendevo

What it is
A social app for real-world plans
Who it's for
People looking for things to do and people to do them with, and the organizers who host them
Where
Launching in Lagos in October 2026 on iPhone, Android and the web

Wendevo helps people find events, communities and others who share their interests, then make plans to meet in person.

  • Discover what's on and buy tickets in a few taps.
  • See who else is going and meet verified people before the day.
  • Organizers sell tickets, check guests in and get paid in one place.
